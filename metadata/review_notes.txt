1. Screen recording

I attached a recording from a physical iPhone on the latest iOS. It starts at launch and walks the usual path:

Cold launch → four onboarding pages: “Set down the book you are holding”, “Write a slip at this page”, “The book can leave. The slips stay.”, and “Reread is a verdict.” Tap Next through them (or Skip), then Open the shelf.

On Shelf, if it is empty you see “The shelf is empty” and tap Set down a book. Enter a Title and Page count, then Set down on the shelf. Tap Write a slip (or a held spine). Set the page with the slider, type a line in “Write the slip at this page”, and tap Pin this slip.

Tap Mark this book gone, confirm on Hollow spine, then reread the frozen sheaf and toggle Reread or Not yet. Open Quotes to Search your slips and use the same verdict. Open Dashboard for gone sheaves, reread slips, hollow spines, and latest slips. Tap the gear for Settings — Re-run onboarding, Reset all data, and Contact Margent.

There is no account, so there is no registration, login, or account deletion. Notes stay on this device and are never shared with other users, so there is no report or block flow. There are no paid features.

2. Purpose and audience

Margent is a commonplace book for people who actually read and then lose the line they meant to keep. Highlights pile up and never get opened again. Here you set down the book you are holding, pin a slip to the page you are on, and keep going. When the copy is returned, sold, given, or lost, you mark it gone — the spine hollows, the sheaf freezes, and the slips stay searchable on this device. Reread is a yes or no, not a star rating. It is for readers, not a bookstore and not a social network.

3. How to try the main features

No login and no demo account. No sample files.

Launch the app. Tap Next through onboarding, or Skip, then Open the shelf.
Set down a book (any title and page count is fine — I used a paperback I had on the desk).
Tap Write a slip, move the page, type a line, Pin this slip.
Open Quotes if you want to search what you wrote.
Mark this book gone when you are done with that copy, then open the hollow spine to reread.
Dashboard just counts what you already did.
Settings → Contact Margent opens the support page if you need us.

Reset all data in Settings if you want a clean shelf again.

4. External services

The reading work itself is on the device. Books, slips, and reread verdicts do not come from a catalog or a cloud account. The only thing a person taps that leaves the app is Contact Margent, which opens our support page (https://margent-sheaf.pro/contact-us). No sign-in service, no payments, no AI.

5. Regions

Same app everywhere. No country-specific features or content. Page numbers and counts follow the device’s number format; day stamps follow the device calendar. Copy is English.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material

Not applicable. This is a personal reading notebook. We are not in a licensed industry and we do not ship anyone else’s protected catalog or media. What you type is yours and stays on this device.
